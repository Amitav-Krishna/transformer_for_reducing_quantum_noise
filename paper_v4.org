* Outline

** Claim (1-2 sentences)

Transformers outperform MLPs for quantum density matrix denoising, and this advantage is not explained by differences in parameter count or network depth. The gap persists as system size scales from 5 to 8 qubits.

** Metrics & Data Protocol

- *Primary metric*: Uhlmann fidelity $F(\rho, \sigma) = (\text{Tr}[\sqrt{\sqrt{\rho} \sigma \sqrt{\rho}}])^2$ --- the quantum-native measure of state similarity
- *Training loss*: Frobenius fidelity (differentiable proxy, correlates with Uhlmann at $\rho=0.99$)
- *Dataset*: 100k (noisy, clean) density matrix pairs
  - 5-qubit: $32 \times 32$ matrices, 5 noise types $\times$ 4 levels = 20 cells, 5000 samples/cell
  - 8-qubit: $256 \times 256$ matrices, same noise distribution
- *Noise types*: depolarizing, amplitude damping, phase damping, bit-flip, mixed
- *Noise levels*: $p \in \{0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20\}$
- *Split*: 80/10/10 train/val/test, seed=42
- *Precision*: float64 throughout (numerical stability for eigendecomposition)
